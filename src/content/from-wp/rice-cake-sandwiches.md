# Open Chicken Rice Cake Sandwiches

- status: publish
- date: 2015-06-25
- slug: chicken-rice-cake-sandwiches
- source: wordpress sql, voice is Mariah first-person

These are a yummy, quick sandwich that can be used as a snack or even a meal if you eat several of them. When I made them the chicken was still hot, but you can also make it with cold chicken.

Ingredients:
 
	 Chicken Breast 
	 Rice Cakes 
	 Mustard 
	 Cottage Cheese 
 
Directions:

Cook your chicken however you like it. You can bake it, grill it, fry it. I cooked mine stove top. A little bit of olive oil into a flat pan, chicken at room-temperature on top of that, then spice your chicken. I did salt, pepper, marjoram, and paprika. Again, you can spice your chicken with whatever spices you like. You can also use leftover chicken. Once the chicken was cooked I let it rest as long as it cooked, keeping the meat juicy.

Take your rice cake and spread the mustard on it.
 [lightbox thumbnail="http://fitfambam.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_0034.jpg" width="400" height="292" align="center"]http://fitfambam.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_0034.jpg[/lightbox]


Next, add a dollop of dry cottage cheese and spread that as well. You can also do the cottage cheese first then the mustard if that's easier.

[lightbox thumbnail="http://fitfambam.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_0035.jpg" width="400" height="292" float="center"]http://fitfambam.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_0035.jpg[/lightbox]

Finally, slice your chicken breast and add it on top.

[lightbox thumbnail="http://fitfambam.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_0037.jpg" width="400" height="292" float="center"]http://fitfambam.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/IMG_0037.jpg[/lightbox]

Done and done! A simple, healthy meal that gives great flavor and can be enjoyed any time of the day. For a snack eat 2 and for a whole meal you can have 4.
