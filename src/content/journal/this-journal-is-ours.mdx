This site belongs to both of us.

Mariah wrote here in 2015, from Slovenia, with a baby in the house and California still close behind her. Those pieces stay. They are hers. I am not going to rewrite them as if they were mine.

I am putting the journal back up because the name is still ours, the house is fuller now, and I want a place that can hold a true sentence.

I will write when I have something worth keeping. Not before. No programme. No invented week.

If you are here from the old site: welcome back. If you are new: we are Luka and Mariah Dolenc. We live in Slovenia with our four children. The rest is in the archive, and in whatever I manage to say plainly later.
