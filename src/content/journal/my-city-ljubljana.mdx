This piece was left as a draft in 2015. We are publishing it as Mariah wrote it.

When Luka and I were married in 2011 I moved the next day to Slovenia and have been here ever since. When we were married we said, "We will live in Ljubljana for one year and after one year we will evaluate how we feel about staying or moving to California." That was four years ago. It's now been five years since my first visit and I love this little city.
